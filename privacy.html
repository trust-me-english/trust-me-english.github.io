Privacy Policy

Trust Me English · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Русская версия

This policy explains what personal data Trust Me English collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Trust Me English iOS app (the “app”) and the tool we use to publish our own videos on social networks.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Aliaksandr Chuyeu, Poland.

For any question about your data, write to trustmeenglish.official@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account

  • A random user ID, created the first time you open the app. You can learn without giving us your name or email.
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google: the account ID that provider gives us, your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address), and your name if you share it.

Learning

  • Your native language and the language you learn.
  • Your learning activity: which cards you studied, your answers, review history, progress and the time of your learning sessions. We use it to choose your next cards and show your progress.

Subscription

  • Payments are processed by Apple. We receive only whether you have an active subscription and its details needed to unlock premium content. We never see your card or bank details.

Technical data

  • When the app talks to our server, the server records standard technical information: IP address, time of the request, app version and error details. We use it to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app: your account, settings, progress and subscription status (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working: technical data and logs (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting for purchases (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Where your data is stored and who processes it

  • Our servers are hosted by OVH SAS (OVHcloud) in data centers in the European Union. OVHcloud stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes.
  • Apple processes payments and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple. Google processes Sign in with Google. They act under their own privacy policies.

We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area, except where you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, whose processing is governed by their own policies.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and learning data: for as long as your account exists.
  • After you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, accounting records).

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all your learning data in the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also ask us by email at trustmeenglish.official@gmail.com.

Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription by itself. To cancel it, use the subscription settings of your Apple ID.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Write to trustmeenglish.official@gmail.com and we will answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl), or to the authority in your country.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Publishing our videos on social networks

We use our own internal tool to publish Trust Me English educational videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram through their official APIs. The tool:

  • connects only to accounts owned by Trust Me English;
  • uploads our videos and their titles, descriptions and tags, and reads the status of those uploads;
  • does not access, collect or store data about other users of these platforms;
  • keeps the access tokens on our own systems and uses them only for these uploads.

When you watch or interact with our videos on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram, that platform’s own privacy policy applies.

10. Security

Data between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our servers is limited to us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change matters for how we use your data, we will tell you in the app.
